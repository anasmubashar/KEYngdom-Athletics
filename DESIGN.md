## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-performance athletic rigor, architectural precision, and collegiate-grade pedigree. Built for an elite youth sports and athletic development brand, the interface projects the discipline of an Olympic training facility paired with the prestige of an Ivy League or Division I institution. It rejects fleeting fitness trends, neon gimmicks, and generic gym aesthetics in favor of commanding geometry, restrained metallic accents, and stark high-contrast typography.

The visual style blends **High-Contrast Modernism** with **Architectural Brutalism**:
- Deep obsidian and charcoal backdrops evoke the heavy iron and disciplined focus of high-performance facilities.
- Metallic champagne and imperial gold accents denote achievement, championship standards, and earned prestige.
- Monolithic, structural grids and sharp-cornered containers communicate uncompromising physical structure, kinetic strength, and technical mastery.
- Subtle hairline borders and refined stat tracking components evoke professional biomechanical analytics and athletic tracking gear.

## Colors

The color palette is rooted in an unyielding dark foundation paired with disciplined metallic finishes and crisp tactical whites.

### Primary & Metallic Accents
- **Primary Gold (`#D4AF37`)**: The cornerstone medal accent. Used for primary CTAs, active indicators, critical tier-1 performance achievements, and key stat callouts.
- **Secondary Gold (`#E5C158`)**: A brightened gold highlight used for hover states, focused rings, and visual accents against dark glass.
- **Tertiary Deep Gold (`#B89628`)**: Burnished antique gold for borders, disabled metallic states, and subdued contextual badges.

### Obsidian Neutrals & Canvas Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0B0C`)**: Void obsidian black representing absolute discipline and high contrast.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#121214`)**: Foundational card and module background.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1E1E24`)**: Elevated interactive surfaces, secondary badges, and data tables.
- **Border / Structural Stroke (`#2A2A32`)**: Crisp dividing grid lines and low-contrast borders.
- **Subdued Text / Meta (`#A1A1AA`)**: High-legibility technical graphite for labels, metric units, and secondary notes.
- **High-Contrast Text (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure athletic white for headers, primary data values, and active interface items.

## Typography

Typography establishes an authoritative collegiate-varsity presence backed by clinical scientific measurement. 

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy (`Barlow Condensed`)**: Uppercase, tight, industrial, and assertive. Reminiscent of championship banners, fieldhouse signage, and uniform numbers. Used for all primary section entries, stat scores, and performance metrics.
- **Body & Subtitles (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: An exacting, modern geometric sans-serif that balances the raw tension of Barlow Condensed with neutral, flawless readability for performance breakdowns, curriculum briefs, and scout notes.
- **Technical & Metric Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Monospaced timing readouts, laser 40-yard splits, heart rate variability metrics, and athlete ID badges. Always uppercase with positive tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a disciplined 12-column architectural grid reflecting structural field lines and weight-room cadence. 

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12-column grid with `3rem` (48px) margins and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Complex dashboards and performance rosters utilize rigid horizontal panels flanked by vertical data splitters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Metric cards stack into dual-column modules.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Edge-to-edge structural cards with condensed vertical gaps (`space-md`) maintain high density for on-field utility.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Governed strictly by multiples of 4px and 8px. Outer containers utilize intentional asymmetric tension, pairing expansive top/bottom section padding (`space-xl` and above) with dense, calibrated internal module gaps (`space-xs` to `space-md`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects soft, cloud-like drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Surface Layering** and **Precision Hairline Borders**.

- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas - `#0B0B0C`)**: The unlit void. Hosts fixed structural frames, canvas navigation, and stadium-grade hero sections.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation - `#121214`)**: Elevated surface bounded by a 1px solid stroke of `#2A2A32`. Hovering or active states shift the border to a metallic hairline gold (`#D4AF37` at 40%–100% opacity).
- **Level 2 (Active Focus & Flyouts - `#1E1E24`)**: Modals, stat drawer pullouts, and dropdown panels. Elevation is accentuated by an ambient, low-spread dark rim shadow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)` combined with a sharp 1px top border of `#D4AF37`.
- **Metallic Edge Embellishment**: High-tier milestone cards and elite performance indicators feature a subtle gold gradient border using `linear-gradient(135deg, #D4AF37 0%, #2A2A32 50%, #B89628 100%)`.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is deliberately **Sharp (`0`)**. 

- Absolute 0px corner radii are enforced across all core interactive elements: buttons, input fields, athletic cards, badges, and modals.
- Sharp geometry projects unyielding resolve, structural discipline, and architectural authority.
- Angled cuts (45-degree chamfered corners of 8px to 12px) may be applied exclusively to category badges, athlete rank tags, and flagship CTA terminal edges to mimic technical varsity and military insignia.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Athletic Button**: 0px radius, solid `#D4AF37` fill, black text (`#0B0B0C`), uppercase `Barlow Condensed` (bold, tracked +0.08em). 48px standard height, padding: `0 24px`. Hover state shifts fill to `#E5C158` with a subtle inset 1px white rim.
- **Secondary Ghost Button**: 0px radius, transparent background, 1px solid `#D4AF37`, gold text (`#D4AF37`). Hover introduces a 10% gold background fill (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`).
- **Tactical Utility Button**: 0px radius, surface `#1E1E24`, 1px solid `#2A2A32`, white text. Used for filter options, pagination, and secondary roster actions.

### Cards & Stat Blocks
- **Performance Metric Card**: Charcoal base (`#121214`) with a 1px border of `#2A2A32`. Card header features an uppercase `label-technical` unit indicator in `#A1A1AA`. The metric value is rendered in oversized `stat-numeric` pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a micro-label suffix in gold (`#D4AF37`).
- **Athlete Profile Card**: Strict rectangular frame. High-contrast monochrome athlete photography with an obsidian-to-transparent gradient overlay, anchored by a bottom metallic nameplate with a gold accent bar (2px width).

### Chips & Stat Badges
- **Status Badges**: 0px radius, strictly uppercase `label-athletic` typography. Built with a dark tinted fill (`#1E1E24`) and a 1px crisp outline corresponding to status (Gold `#D4AF37` for Tier-1/Division-Ready; Slate `#2A2A32` for Baseline/In-Progress).
- **Metric Micro-Chips**: `JetBrains Mono`, 11px, with high-density padding (`2px 8px`), displaying vertical jump, 40-yard dash, or strength metrics.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields**: 44px height, rectangular, background `#121214`, 1px solid `#2A2A32`. Text in `#FFFFFF` with placeholder in `#A1A1AA`. Focus state transitions the border to solid `#D4AF37` with no glow or halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Strictly squared (0px radius). Unchecked: `#121214` surface with 1px `#2A2A32` border. Checked: `#D4AF37` solid background with `#0B0B0C` geometric tick/dot indicator.

### Lists & Leaderboards
- **Roster & Performance Rows**: Horizontal tabular bands with 1px bottom border `#1E1E24`. Hover highlights row with `#121214` and illuminates the rank index number from muted gray to vibrant `#D4AF37`.
- **Rank Indicator**: Boxed mono-spaced numeric display (`01`, `02`, `03`) positioned in a locked 36x36px square with right-aligned technical timing data.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **The Crucible Tier Meter**: Segmented progress bar composed of 5 discrete rectangular blocks (2px gap between blocks). Active blocks fill with metallic gold; incomplete blocks retain dark graphite `#1E1E24`.
- **Scout Evaluation Radar Box**: Encased inside a sharp-edged container featuring technical coordinate crosshairs at all four corners, displaying combine analytics and physical benchmarking vectors.
